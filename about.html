Our Story

From a small studio to a global agency

NexaWeb was founded in 2012 by a pair of designers who believed the web deserved better — more intentional, more human, more effective. What started as two people working from a shared desk has grown into a team of over 30 specialists spanning design, engineering, strategy, and growth.

We've partnered with start-ups in seed stage and Fortune 500 enterprises alike. What unites every engagement is our commitment to excellence: we never ship something we're not proud of, and we never stop pushing the work to be better.

Today, NexaWeb operates from offices in New York, London, and Sydney — and collaborates with talent across six continents.

What Drives Us

Our core principles

The beliefs that shape every decision we make — from strategy through to the final pixel.

01

Craft Over Commodity

We refuse to ship mediocre work. Every project receives the same level of care and attention, regardless of budget or scale. Quality is non-negotiable.

02

Strategy Informs Design

Beautiful without purpose is decoration. We root every design decision in research, data, and a deep understanding of your audience and goals.

03

Transparency Always

No surprises. We communicate clearly, price fairly, and keep you informed at every stage. Your trust is the foundation of everything we do together.

04

Performance Matters

A slow, inaccessible website is a liability. We build for speed, accessibility, and reliability — every time, without exception.

05

Long-Term Partnership

We don't disappear after launch. We measure success by your growth over time, not by project closure. Your wins are our wins.

06

Continuous Evolution

The digital landscape never stops changing. Neither do we. We invest continuously in learning, tooling, and process so you always benefit from best practice.
